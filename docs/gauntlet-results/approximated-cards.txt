# Approximated ("GAP'd") cards — fidelity worklist
#
# These cards RESOLVE (so decks containing them count as "playable" in the
# coverage snapshot) but are modeled with a deliberate simplification. A deck's
# true fidelity is lower than its coverage suggests; this is the input to a
# planned per-deck fidelity/GAP-card score. Most simplifications weaken
# non-aggro decks, compounding the material-referee bias.
#
# Format: <card> — <what's approximated>

# --- creatures ---
Tarmogoyf — fixed 4/5; dynamic */1+* (card types in all graveyards, a Layer-7a CDA) not modeled
Steel Leaf Champion — "can't be blocked by creatures with power 2 or less" evasion elided
Soul-Scar Mage — Prowess modeled; the "-1/-1 counters instead of noncombat damage" replacement elided
Torbran, Thane of Red Fell — vanilla 2/4; the "+2 to red damage" replacement elided
# DONE (fidelity grind): Empyrean Eagle (flying anthem), Supreme Phantom (Spirit anthem),
# Hangarback Walker (death-Thopters + {1},{T} pump), Winding Constrictor (counter replacement)

# --- vehicles (Crew is real; secondary abilities GAP'd) ---
Heart of Kiran — Crew 3 + Flying/Vigilance real; planeswalker-loyalty alternate crew elided
Aethersphere Harvester — Crew 1 + Flying real; energy + lifelink ability elided
# DONE (fidelity grind): Smuggler's Copter (attack/block loot trigger)

# --- spells ---
Dig Through Time — modeled as draw 2; the look-at-7-and-select-2 dig elided (delve is real)
Wild Slash — 2 damage real; Ferocious "can't be prevented" elided
# DONE (fidelity grind): Fatal Push (Revolt MV<=4 via permanent_left_battlefield_this_turn)
Boros Charm — 4-to-player + double-strike modes real; "indestructible to all your permanents" mode is a no-op; planeswalker target elided
Censor — counter-unless-{1} real; Cycling elided
Syncopate — counter-unless-{X} real; the "exile if countered" rider elided
Stubborn Denial — counter-unless-{1} real; Ferocious upgrade to {3} elided
Once Upon a Time — {2}{G} dig real; free-first-spell-of-the-game alt-cost elided

# --- lands ---
(shocklands) — "pay 2 life or enters tapped" resolved by a fixed policy (pay if life > cost), not a player choice
Castle Garenbrig — restricted ramp uses OnlyCastCreatureSpells; "or activate abilities of creatures" half elided
Raging Ravine — animates to 3/3; the attack-trigger +1/+1 counter elided
Creeping Tar Pit — animates to 3/2; "can't be blocked" elided
Field of the Dead — taps for {C}; the 7-differently-named-lands Zombie trigger elided
Blast Zone — taps for {C}; charge-counter board-wipe elided
